## 1. Services Provided
The Provider agrees to grant the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access and use the **Ozzyl Hospital Management System (HMS)** for their internal hospital and clinic management.

The core modules provided in this agreement include:
*   **Hospital Website:** A professional, responsive basic website for the hospital.
*   **Reception & Patient Management:** Patient registration, visit management, admission management, and invoicing.
*   **Laboratory & Radiology Module:** Test entry, payment, queue management, result generation, and history.
*   **Pharmacy & Inventory Module:** Stock management, supplier tracking, batch/expiry alerts, and pharmacy sales.
*   **Administration & Finance Module:** Daily income/expense tracking, staff payments, shareholder profit distribution, and accounting reports.

*Modifications or custom feature requests beyond these core modules will be subject to separate scoping and additional fees.*

## 5. Data Ownership and Security

### 5.1 Administrative & Operational Data
All financial data, stock information, staff details, and operational/administrative records entered into the system remain the sole and exclusive property of the Client (Hospital).

### 5.2 Patient Health Records (Patient-Centric Model)
In accordance with the **Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025** of the Government of Bangladesh and the **National Digital Health Strategy 2023-2027**:
*   Patient health information (test reports, prescriptions, etc.) is classified as "Sensitive Personal Data," and its ultimate owner is the patient themselves.
*   The Client (Hospital) serves as an authorized Custodian of this data while providing treatment.
*   The Provider ensures a modern and seamless mechanism through which patients can review and store their medical history in their personal "Ozzylife" accounts. This is compliant with the government's **Data Interoperability** and **Health Information Exchange (HIE)** policy framework.

### 5.3 De-Identified Data Usage
The Provider may use fully **de-identified/anonymized** statistical data from the system — from which no individual, patient, or institution can be identified — for the purposes of platform improvement, service quality enhancement, and aggregated health analytics.

### 5.4 Data Security
The Provider will utilize standard cloud security measures (including Cloudflare Zero Trust, automated backups, and database encryption) to protect all system data. In accordance with the **Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025**, the Provider will notify the Client within 24 hours in the event of any data breach.

### 5.5 Data Transfer (Upon Termination)
Upon termination of this Agreement, provided all outstanding dues have been cleared, the Provider will transfer the Client's administrative and operational data (in CSV/SQL format) digitally within thirty (30) days. Patient health records will remain safely accessible to the respective patients via their personal Ozzylife accounts, as is their civic right.
