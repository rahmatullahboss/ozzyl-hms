# Doctor-Specific Protected Commission Floor Design

Date: 2026-07-25
Status: Approved product direction; implementation pending written-spec review
Domain: Billing, doctor commission, discount allocation, payout, finance reporting

## 1. Problem

Hospitals can configure a doctor to receive a commission such as 25% of the canonical commission base. When the doctor authorizes a patient discount, the current system can consume the doctor's full earned commission as a doctor waiver. Some hospitals instead have an agreement that only part of the commission is waiver-eligible.

Example agreement:

- Doctor commission rate: 25%
- Maximum doctor-funded waiver rate: 20% of the same commission base
- Protected doctor commission rate: 5% of the same commission base

The doctor may authorize a 30%, 50%, or larger patient discount, but the system must never consume the protected 5% as doctor waiver. Any discount beyond the doctor's waiver capacity is hospital-funded.

The protected amount is a floor against doctor-authorized waiver. It is not an unconditional payment when the underlying service is cancelled, refunded, reversed, or otherwise becomes ineligible for commission.

## 2. Existing System Context

The existing commission system already provides:

- Doctor-specific and service-specific commission rules.
- Percentage and flat commission rule types.
- Effective start and end dates.
- Doctor waiver allocation separated from hospital-funded discount.
- Accrual fields for earned commission, doctor waiver, payable commission, paid amount, and balance.
- Billing preview and accrual creation flows.

Current waiver calculation caps the doctor waiver at the full earned commission. Therefore, a fully waived commission can produce zero payable commission. The new design adds a configurable waiver policy and protected floor to the same doctor/service commission rule.

## 3. Research-Backed Design Principles

The design follows patterns used by mature incentive-compensation systems:

1. **Effective-dated rule versions**: a newly effective financial rule creates a new version instead of overwriting the historical rule.
2. **Locked historical results**: historical or paid commission results are not silently recalculated after a rule change.
3. **Calculation traceability**: every payout can be explained from commission base through earned, protected, waived, payable, paid, and balance amounts.
4. **Audit history**: the system records who changed or approved a rule, when it changed, and which version was used.
5. **Approval and freeze states**: financially material changes and finalized payouts can be reviewed and locked.
6. **Adjustments instead of mutation**: corrections after settlement are posted as reversals or adjustments, not edits to paid history.

These principles are consistent with official SAP Incentive Management guidance on object versions and effective dates, Salesforce Spiff guidance on effective dating, audit trails and frozen statements, and Oracle Incentive Compensation guidance on approvals and immutable paid batches.

## 4. Product Decision

Protected commission is configured **per doctor commission rule**. A rule can already be scoped by doctor, service type, incentive type, test, or category, so the waiver policy inherits the same scope.

The calculation source of truth is always the resolved commission rule and the immutable snapshot stored on the commission accrual. A hospital-level default may prefill the rule editor, but it must not be a hidden runtime fallback that can change historical calculations.

### 4.1 Supported waiver policies

Each doctor commission rule has one of three explicit policies:

- `full_earned`: legacy behavior; the doctor can waive up to the full earned commission.
- `protected_floor`: the doctor can waive only the amount above a configured protected floor.
- `no_doctor_waiver`: none of the earned commission can fund a patient discount.

This explicit policy is preferable to inferring behavior from a zero value because zero can otherwise mean either “no floor” or “not configured.”

### 4.2 Backward compatibility

All existing commission rules migrate to `full_earned`. Existing bills and accruals are not recalculated. Hospitals may enable protected floors on new effective-dated rule versions.

No current hospital payout may change merely because the software was upgraded.

## 5. Terminology

- **Canonical commission base**: the amount on which the resolved commission rule is calculated after existing base rules, performer reserve, eligible base-reducing effects, and rounding.
- **Earned commission**: commission generated by the resolved rule before doctor waiver.
- **Protected commission floor**: the minimum portion of earned commission that a doctor-authorized waiver cannot consume.
- **Doctor waiver capacity**: earned commission minus protected commission.
- **Requested doctor waiver**: the patient discount amount explicitly allocated to the doctor.
- **Applied doctor waiver**: the amount actually consumed from the doctor's earned commission.
- **Hospital-funded overflow**: requested doctor waiver that exceeds the doctor's remaining waiver capacity.
- **Payable commission**: earned commission minus applied doctor waiver.

## 6. Calculation Contract

For one commission accrual line:

```text
B = canonical commission base
E = earned commission
F = protected commission floor
C = maximum doctor waiver capacity
R = requested doctor waiver allocated to this doctor/line
A = applied doctor waiver
P = payable commission
H = hospital-funded overflow

C = max(0, E - F)
A = min(max(0, R), C)
P = E - A
H = max(0, R - A)
```

Invariants:

```text
0 <= F <= E
0 <= A <= C <= E
P >= F
E = A + P
R = A + H
```

### 6.1 Percentage commission rules

For a percentage commission rule:

```text
E = round(B × commission_rate)
F = round(B × protected_rate)
```

Validation:

```text
0 <= protected_rate <= commission_rate
```

The maximum doctor-funded waiver rate is derived:

```text
maximum_waiver_rate = commission_rate - protected_rate
```

Only `protected_rate` is stored as an independent business value. Storing both protected rate and maximum waiver rate would introduce two editable values that can contradict each other.

### 6.2 Flat commission rules

For a flat commission rule:

```text
E = configured flat commission, subject to existing earning eligibility
F = configured protected flat amount
```

Validation:

```text
0 <= protected_flat_amount <= flat_commission_amount
```

## 7. Scope and Precedence

The protected floor follows the exact commission rule selected by the existing rule-resolution process.

Typical specificity order remains:

1. Doctor + service + incentive type + exact test.
2. Doctor + service + incentive type + category.
3. Doctor + service + incentive type default.
4. Existing fallback behavior, where supported.

The waiver policy must not be resolved independently from the commission rule. Separating them could select a commission rate from one rule and a waiver floor from another, creating an invalid contract.

## 8. Multiple Bill Lines and Multiple Doctors

A bill may contain several commissionable lines and more than one doctor. Doctor waiver allocations remain doctor-specific.

For each doctor:

1. Resolve and calculate every eligible accrual line.
2. Calculate each line's earned commission, protected floor, and waiver capacity.
3. Consume the doctor's requested waiver deterministically in stable bill-line order.
4. Never consume more than a line's waiver capacity.
5. Carry unused requested waiver to the next eligible line for the same doctor.
6. Convert any final excess to hospital-funded discount.

The allocation order must be deterministic and covered by tests so recalculation produces the same result.

Rounding residue, when present, is assigned to the final eligible line for that doctor so totals reconcile exactly.

## 9. Important Business Boundaries

### 9.1 Protected against waiver, not against reversal

Protected commission applies only to doctor-funded discount. It does not prevent a proportional reversal or clawback when:

- The bill or service is cancelled.
- The service is refunded or returned.
- The service was entered incorrectly.
- The earning becomes ineligible.
- A financial correction reverses the original accrual.

### 9.2 Hospital discount is not doctor waiver

A normal hospital discount, charity discount, management discount, corporate discount, or other hospital-funded benefit does not reduce doctor commission unless a separate, explicit commission-base policy says it reduces the canonical commission base.

A receptionist must not be able to label an arbitrary hospital discount as doctor waiver after the bill is finalized.

### 9.3 Payout deductions are separate

TDS, advance deduction, payout adjustment, and other settlement deductions are not doctor waiver. They must remain separate statement lines and accounting effects.

### 9.4 Zero-payable accruals remain auditable

When a legacy `full_earned` rule produces zero payable commission, the system must still store the accrual with earned, waived, and payable values. It must not skip the row. Otherwise, reporting loses evidence that commission was earned and fully waived.

## 10. Data Model

### 10.1 `doctor_commission_rules`

Add rule-level fields:

```text
waiver_policy TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'full_earned'
protected_rate_bps INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
protected_flat_amount REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
rule_version INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 1
supersedes_rule_id INTEGER NULL
approval_status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'approved'
approved_by INTEGER NULL
approved_at TEXT NULL
```

Constraints:

```text
waiver_policy IN ('full_earned', 'protected_floor', 'no_doctor_waiver')
protected_rate_bps >= 0
protected_flat_amount >= 0
```

Application validation additionally enforces:

- Percentage rule: protected rate cannot exceed commission rate.
- Flat rule: protected amount cannot exceed flat commission.
- `full_earned`: protected value is treated as zero.
- `no_doctor_waiver`: protected floor equals the full earned commission at calculation time.
- Effective ranges for competing rules cannot overlap at the same specificity.

### 10.2 `doctor_commission_accruals`

Store an immutable calculation snapshot:

```text
commission_base_amount REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
waiver_policy_snapshot TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'full_earned'
protected_rate_bps_snapshot INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
protected_flat_amount_snapshot REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
protected_commission_amount REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
maximum_waiver_amount REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
requested_waiver_amount REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
hospital_funded_overflow_amount REAL NOT NULL DEFAULT 0
```

Existing fields remain authoritative for the monetary bridge:

```text
earned_commission_amount
doctor_waiver_amount
payable_commission_amount
paid_amount
balance_amount
waiver_allocation_id
```

The snapshot prevents a later rule edit from changing the meaning of an old accrual.

### 10.3 Rule versioning

A financially material change to an active or already-used rule creates a new row:

- End-date the old version immediately before the new effective date.
- Insert the new version with `rule_version + 1`.
- Link it through `supersedes_rule_id`.
- Preserve the old row for audit and historical calculation tracing.

Draft rules that have never been used may be edited in place.

Backdated changes that overlap posted or paid accruals are blocked from normal editing. They require an explicit correction workflow that creates reversals and replacement accruals.

## 11. API Behavior

### 11.1 Commission rule API

Create/update payload adds:

```text
waiverPolicy
protectedRate
protectedFlatAmount
newEffectiveFrom
```

Responses return both stored and derived values:

```text
commissionRate
protectedRate
maximumWaiverRate
waiverPolicy
ruleVersion
effectiveFrom
effectiveTo
approvalStatus
```

The update endpoint must distinguish:

- Nonfinancial metadata edit.
- Draft financial edit.
- New effective-dated version.
- Invalid overlapping version.
- Backdated change requiring correction workflow.

### 11.2 Doctor waiver preview API

The preview returns a complete explanation:

```text
commissionBaseAmount
earnedCommissionAmount
protectedCommissionAmount
maximumDoctorWaiverAmount
requestedDoctorWaiverAmount
appliedDoctorWaiverAmount
hospitalFundedOverflowAmount
payableCommissionAmount
waiverPolicy
commissionRuleId
ruleVersion
lines[]
```

The server performs the authoritative calculation. The client may display an estimate but must never invent the final split.

### 11.3 Finalization

At bill finalization:

1. Re-run the authoritative calculation using the bill date and final lines.
2. Validate that the commission rule version is still applicable.
3. Persist bill discount allocations.
4. Persist all commission accrual snapshots, including zero-payable accruals.
5. Persist audit/accounting events idempotently.

Preview and finalization must use the same shared calculation utility.

## 12. User Interface

### 12.1 Commission rule editor

For a percentage rule:

```text
Commission rate: 25%
Doctor waiver policy: Protected commission floor
Protected commission rate: 5%
Maximum doctor-funded waiver: 20% (calculated, read-only)
```

For a flat rule:

```text
Flat commission: ৳250
Doctor waiver policy: Protected commission floor
Protected commission amount: ৳50
Maximum doctor-funded waiver: ৳200 (calculated, read-only)
```

The editor shows a live example and blocks invalid combinations.

### 12.2 Billing waiver preview

When doctor waiver is selected, show:

```text
Earned commission
Protected commission
Maximum doctor contribution
Doctor-funded amount
Hospital-funded overflow
Final doctor payable
```

Do not show only “eligible commission,” because that does not explain how much is protected.

### 12.3 High-risk warnings

Require explicit acknowledgement or approval when:

- A requested doctor waiver exceeds doctor capacity.
- Hospital-funded overflow crosses the hospital's discount approval threshold.
- A user attempts a backdated financial rule change.
- A new rule version overlaps another active rule.

## 13. Authorization, Approval, and Audit

Minimum release controls:

- Only commission-admin roles can create or version rules.
- Every financial change records before/after values, user, timestamp, tenant, and effective date.
- Paid settlements are immutable.
- Corrections use adjustment/reversal records.

Optional hospital setting:

```text
Require approval for commission rule changes
```

When enabled:

- A creator saves a draft version.
- A different authorized MD/hospital-admin approves it.
- Only approved versions participate in calculation.

This maker-checker workflow should be configurable because small hospitals may not have two separate authorized users.

## 14. Reporting and Explainability

Doctor commission statements and admin reports must expose this bridge:

```text
Commission base
Commission rate / flat rule
Earned commission
Protected commission
Maximum waiver capacity
Doctor waiver applied
Payable commission
Paid amount
Remaining balance
Hospital-funded overflow related to doctor waiver
Rule version and effective date
```

Reports must distinguish:

- Doctor waiver.
- Hospital discount.
- Commission-base reduction.
- Payout adjustment or deduction.
- Refund/reversal/clawback.

The statement should be frozen once paid. Later corrections appear as new adjustment lines rather than rewriting the paid statement.

## 15. Failure Handling

- Missing rule: no commission accrual; return a traceable reason.
- Invalid protected configuration: block rule activation and bill finalization using that rule.
- Rule overlap: block activation.
- Stale preview: finalization recalculates and returns a conflict if the result changed materially.
- Duplicate finalization request: idempotency prevents duplicate accruals and accounting events.
- Partial write failure: transaction/compensating behavior must prevent bill allocation and commission accrual from disagreeing.

## 16. Test Matrix

### 16.1 Unit tests

- 25% commission, 5% floor, no waiver.
- Waiver below capacity.
- Waiver exactly at capacity.
- Waiver above capacity.
- 100% protected / no-doctor-waiver behavior.
- 0% protected / full-earned behavior.
- Flat commission with protected amount.
- Invalid protected rate greater than commission rate.
- Invalid protected flat amount greater than flat commission.
- Decimal and rounding boundaries.
- Multiple lines with deterministic allocation.
- Multiple doctors with isolated waiver pools.
- Zero-payable accrual still persisted.

### 16.2 Integration tests

- Preview and finalization return identical monetary results.
- Rule version selected by bill date.
- Old bill remains tied to old rule snapshot after a new version is activated.
- Overlapping effective ranges rejected.
- Backdated change over paid accrual rejected.
- Excess waiver becomes hospital-funded allocation.
- Refund creates reversal without violating historical snapshot.
- Duplicate bill finalization does not duplicate accruals.

### 16.3 Reporting tests

- Earned = waiver + payable.
- Requested waiver = doctor-funded + hospital-funded overflow.
- Paid + balance reconciles to payable after adjustments.
- Doctor, bill, daily collection, and finance reports use the same canonical payable amount.

- Enable for a selected hospital.

- Do not recalculate historical accruals automatically.

## 18. Acceptance Criteria

The feature is complete when all of the following are true:

1. A hospital can configure different waiver policies and protected floors for different doctors and services.
2. A doctor with a 25% commission and 5% protected rate can never have more than 20% of the same base consumed as doctor waiver.
3. Excess requested waiver is automatically classified as hospital-funded.
4. Historical bills retain their original rule and monetary snapshot after rule changes.
5. Paid payouts cannot be silently rewritten.
6. Preview, accrual, payout, and reports reconcile to the same formulas.
7. Every rule change and payout adjustment is auditable.
8. Existing hospitals retain current behavior until they explicitly activate a protected-floor rule version.
